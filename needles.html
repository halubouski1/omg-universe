Cartridge features

Needel tip

Tip of the cartridge literally affects your work:
– field of view
– ink flow
– tip saturation
– needle whipping (sloped and flat tips)

Flat tip makes the needle coordinated, stable. However you can achieve different needle flexibility by bending it as well as using different needle system (amount of bars)

Vents

Bigger vents give better air circulation inside the cartridge, thereby ink flow starts to be consistent (saturate and release pigment consistently)

  • Needel diameter

    The choice of needle diameter depends on the skin type.

    Thinner needle is more flexible than thicker one. You need to find a balance between the skin thickness and needle diameter.

    Thus thin needle diameter (0.25) will be required for thin skin, resp.

    0.30 needle diameter suits for normal skin type and 0.35 needle diameter for thick skin

    • 0.25mm For thin skin
    • 0.30mm For normal skin
    • 0.35mm For thick skin

The 3RL needle

The 3RL needle consists of 3 needles arranged in a round bundle (round liner). This configuration allows for creating thin, precise lines with minimal skin trauma.

3RL Needle Diameter: The diameter affects the saturation, density of lines, and level of skin trauma. The choice of needle diameter depends on the client’s skin type, technique, and desired result.
