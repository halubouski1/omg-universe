The OMG Universe partner program (the “Program”) is run by OMG OÜ, Viige 11, 13516 Tallinn, Estonia. In short: the buyer gets −10%, the partner gets 5% OMG Credit, the credit is valid for 12 months.

Principle and rewards

Referral code. Every approved partner receives a personal promo code and a referral link. The buyer enters the code in the cart; when the buyer opens the referral link, the code is applied automatically.

Discount for the buyer. 10% on eligible products at the regular retail price.

Reward for the partner. 5% of the amount actually paid for eligible products after the 10% discount, excluding VAT, shipping and any other fees, credited as OMG Credit.

Example (without VAT). Cart €200 → discount €20 → the buyer pays €180 → the partner receives €9 of OMG Credit.

When the credit is added. After the payment is confirmed, the order is completed and the return period has ended. If the order is cancelled or partly or fully refunded, the credit is reduced or cancelled accordingly.

When the code does not apply

Exclusions. Black Friday, sales, promotional and discounted items, bundles with a special price, wholesale / B2B orders and purchases at partner prices.

No stacking. The referral code cannot be combined with other promo codes, the student discount or other special offers.

Mixed cart. The 10% applies only to eligible items; excluded items get no discount and earn no credit.

Fair use. Partners do not earn credit for their own orders. Linked accounts and suspicious transactions are reviewed before any credit is added.

Using OMG Credit

Value. €1 of OMG Credit = €1 off the next eligible purchase.

Where it applies. Only to regular retail products without an active discount. It cannot be used on Black Friday, promotions, discounted items, wholesale / B2B orders, shipping or fees.

Validity. 12 months from the date the credit is added; unused credit expires after that.

Other rules. OMG Credit is not paid out in cash, cannot be transferred to other participants and cannot be used to buy gift cards. If a product paid with credit is returned, the credit used is restored according to the return rules.

Participation

Partners apply through the form on the partner program page. Participation starts after OMG OÜ approves the application. OMG OÜ may decline an application, suspend a partner code or cancel credit obtained in breach of these rules.

Partners see their code, link, referred orders and every credit operation in the partner account.

Changes and contact

OMG OÜ may change these rules; credit already added keeps its value and validity. Questions: omg.permanent@gmail.com.
