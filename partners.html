How the credit is counted

  3. Client pays€180

The credit is 5% of the amount actually paid for eligible products after the 10% discount — without VAT, shipping and other fees. €1 of OMG Credit = €1 off your next eligible purchase.

Good to know

Program conditions

Your code doesn’t apply to

  • Black Friday, sales, promotional and discounted items
  • Bundles with a special price
  • Wholesale / B2B orders and purchases at partner prices
  • It can’t be combined with other promo codes, the student discount or other offers
  • In a mixed cart the 10% applies only to eligible items — excluded items get no discount and earn no credit
  • Your own orders don’t earn credit

OMG Credit — how to use it

  • €1 of credit = €1 off your next purchase of regular-price products
  • Valid for 12 months from the date it is credited; unused credit then expires
  • Credited after the order is paid, completed and the return period is over
  • Not valid on sales, B2B orders, shipping and fees
  • Not paid out in cash, not transferable, not for gift cards
  • Participation after approval; you see every order and credit in your account
